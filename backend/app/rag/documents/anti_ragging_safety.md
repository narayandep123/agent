answer: Ragging, harassment, bullying, threats, violence, discrimination, and retaliation are prohibited. A related complaint or grievance is recorded confidentially and sent to an authorized human officer without the assistant deciding guilt. Immediate danger is escalated to campus security or emergency services.

## Prohibited Conduct
Ragging, coercion, humiliation, bullying, stalking, harassment, threats, violence, discrimination, and retaliation are prohibited in physical and digital campus environments.

## Immediate Safety
Anyone in immediate danger or requiring urgent medical help should contact campus security or local emergency services first. The assistant is not an emergency-response service.

## Reporting
Students, faculty, staff, visitors, and witnesses may report incidents. A useful report includes what happened, when and where, immediate safety needs, and evidence the reporter chooses to provide.

## Confidentiality and Non-Retaliation
Reports are shared only with personnel who need the information for safety, support, or authorized review. Retaliation is prohibited and is treated as a separate urgent concern.

## Human Review
The assistant records and routes each complaint or grievance; it does not determine credibility, guilt, punishment, or legal status. Authorized officers apply due process and communicate permissible updates.

## Support and Appeals
Affected persons may request counselling, accessibility support, academic assistance, or protective measures. Available review or appeal routes accompany the human decision.
