answer: Examination eligibility, conduct, grading, revaluation, and make-up assessments are controlled by the Examination Office. Students may request clarification or revaluation within the published deadline, but the assistant cannot change a grade or decide malpractice cases.

## Eligibility and Admit Cards
Eligibility is determined from registration, attendance, academic standing, and published programme requirements. Admit-card issues are routed to the Examination Office with course and examination details.

## Examination Conduct
Candidates must follow identity, seating, permitted-material, device, and invigilation instructions. Suspected malpractice is documented and referred to an authorized committee; the assistant does not declare guilt or impose penalties.

## Results and Grade Clarification
Published results come only from the official academic record. Clarification requests must identify the course, assessment, and disputed item. Chat calculations are not official grade changes.

## Revaluation
Revaluation or answer-script review must be requested within the deadline announced with results and may require the prescribed fee. The Examination Office communicates the authorized outcome.

## Make-Up Assessment
Students absent for documented exceptional reasons may request a make-up assessment. An authorized academic officer reviews the evidence; approval is not automatic.

## Appeals and Audit
Appeals follow the published academic route. Eligibility, misconduct, revaluation, and grade-change decisions must identify the human decision-maker and remain auditable.
