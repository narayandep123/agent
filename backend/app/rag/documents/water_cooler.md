## Scope
This policy covers the maintenance of drinking water coolers, dispensers, and purification units installed on every floor of academic and hostel blocks.

## Reporting A Faulty Cooler
When a water cooler is not working or is leaking, report the exact block and floor. The floor number is mandatory because each floor unit is serviced independently.

## Service Standard
A non-functional cooler is treated as a low-risk facility ticket and must be repaired or replaced within 24 working hours to maintain safe drinking water access.
