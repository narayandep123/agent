## Scope
This policy covers booking and use of shared computer laboratories and their workstations for practical sessions and self-study.

## Booking Slots
Lab slots are reserved in fixed time blocks and require confirmation before allocation. Priority is given to scheduled academic sessions over individual study.

## Responsible Use
Users must not install unauthorised software or bypass access controls. Equipment faults should be reported through the facilities maintenance process.
