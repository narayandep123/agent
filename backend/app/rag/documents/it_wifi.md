## Scope
This policy covers campus internet, WiFi access, and shared IT equipment such as projectors and classroom computers.

## Reporting IT Faults
Connectivity outages, projector failures, and computer faults are reported as maintenance tickets with the room and floor. IT support resolves them on a best-effort same-day basis.

## Acceptable Use
Network access is for academic use. Sharing credentials or bypassing network controls is prohibited under the Institutional Conduct Policy.
