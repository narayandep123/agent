answer: Fees must be paid through approved channels by published deadlines. Duplicate or failed transactions are investigated using payment evidence. Refunds, waivers, instalments, and financial-hold removal require Finance Office review and cannot be promised by the assistant.

## Approved Payment Channels
Payments must use the authorized portal, bank account, or cashier process. Students should retain the reference and receipt. Staff never request passwords, OTPs, or full card credentials in chat.

## Failed or Duplicate Payments
A report must include the student identifier, amount, date, channel, and transaction reference. Evidence should mask unrelated sensitive information. Finance reconciles the transaction before updating the ledger.

## Due Dates and Holds
Applicable deadlines come from the current programme fee schedule. The assistant cannot waive a charge or remove a financial hold.

## Refunds
Eligibility depends on payment type, withdrawal date, service status, and the published refund schedule. Approved refunds use a verified payment method or bank account and are audited.

## Instalments and Assistance
Students facing hardship may request an instalment plan, extension, or financial-assistance referral. Approval requires authorized human review.

## Disputes and Privacy
Disputes may be escalated with the receipt and correspondence. Financial records are restricted, and suspected fraud or phishing is escalated immediately.
