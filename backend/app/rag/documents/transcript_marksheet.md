## Scope
This policy governs the issuance of official academic transcripts and consolidated marksheets.

## Request And Approval
Transcript requests require identity verification and academic-office approval. Official transcripts are issued in sealed form for external institutions.

## Processing Time
Standard transcripts are issued within five working days. Requests pending fee clearance are held until dues are settled.
