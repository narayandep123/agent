## Scope
This procedure explains how to request a bonafide certificate that confirms a student's current enrolment status.

## Documents Required
To request a bonafide certificate a student needs a valid student ID, the enrolment number, and a stated purpose such as a scholarship or bank account. No fee is charged for the first two copies per semester.

## Approval Flow
The request is verified against enrolment records and approved by the academic office. Bonafide certificates are never auto-issued because they are used for official verification.
