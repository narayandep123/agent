## Scope
This policy governs the reporting and resolution of facility maintenance issues in classrooms, laboratories, hostels, libraries, and common campus areas.

## How To Report
Any student, faculty, or staff member may report a maintenance issue by describing the location, the floor, and the nature of the problem. Reports are converted into tracked service tickets.

## Response Times
Low-risk issues are logged automatically and addressed within 48 working hours. Issues affecting safety, electricity, or water supply are escalated to the facilities supervisor immediately.

## Required Details
A valid maintenance request must include the building or room. For water, cooling, or electrical faults the floor number is also required before a ticket can be created.
