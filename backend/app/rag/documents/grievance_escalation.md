## Scope
This policy defines how unclear, disputed, or out-of-policy requests are escalated to a human officer.

## When To Escalate
If policy evidence is insufficient or the applicable rule is ambiguous, the assistant must escalate to a human grievance officer instead of guessing an outcome.

## Escalation Path
Escalated cases are routed to the relevant department officer with the full request context and audit trail. The requester is informed that a human is reviewing the case.
