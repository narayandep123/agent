## Scope
This policy governs the reservation of study seats and reading-hall desks in campus libraries through the CampusFlow assistant.

## Booking Windows
Seats may be booked from Monday to Saturday between the library operating hours. Bookings are unavailable on Sundays and declared holidays.

## Confirmation Requirement
Every booking must be explicitly confirmed by the requester before a seat is allocated. The assistant proposes availability but never finalises a reservation without confirmation.

## Fair Use
A member may hold one active seat reservation per time slot. Repeated no-shows may lead to temporary suspension of booking privileges.
