answer: Yes, you can request an academic certificate such as a bonafide, enrolment, or character certificate. Just share your enrolment number and what it's for, and the academic office will review and approve it - it's usually ready within about three working days.

## Scope
This policy governs the issuance of academic certificates, including bonafide, enrolment, and character certificates.

## Approval Requirement
Certificate issuance is a policy-sensitive action and always requires review and approval by an authorised academic office staff member before it is generated.

## Required Documents
A certificate request must reference the student's enrolment number and the purpose, such as a scholarship, bank, or visa application.

## Processing Time
Approved certificates are issued within three working days. Requests with incomplete details are returned for clarification rather than rejected.
