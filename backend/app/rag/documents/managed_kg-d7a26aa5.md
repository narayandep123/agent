## Policy Information
Scholarship policies for Scheduled Tribe (ST) students in India generally require applicant proof of ST category status, an annual family income ceiling (typically ₹2.50 lakh to ₹6 lakh depending on the scheme level), regular enrollment in recognized institutions, and academic progression. Applications are processed centrally or via state portals like the National Scholarship Portal. [1, 2, 3, 4]

Key National ST Scholarship Schemes & Criteria
Pre-Matric Scholarship (Classes IX–X)
Category: Scheduled Tribe students studying in classes 9 and 10.
Income Limit: Total annual family income must not exceed ₹2.50 lakh.
Support: Monthly stipends provided for 10 months a year for day scholars and hostellers. [1]

Post-Matric Scholarship (Class XI to Post-Graduation)
Category: Pursuing recognized courses from matriculation/class 10 onwards.
Income Limit: Annual family income limit up to ₹2.50 lakh.
Support: Covers compulsory institutional fees and a monthly maintenance allowance. [1]

Top Class Education for Higher Education
Category: Degree courses in notified premier institutes (like IITs, IIMs).
Income Limit: Family income from all sources must not exceed ₹6.00 lakh per year.
Support: Full tuition fees, non-refundable charges, books, and computer allowances. [1]

National Overseas Scholarship
Category: Master’s, Ph.D., or Post-Doctoral studies abroad.
Academic Standard: Minimum 55% marks in the last qualifying exam.
Income Limit: Annual family income capped at ₹6.00 lakh. [1, 2]
