## Scope
This policy applies to residents, visitors, wardens, and security staff at institution-managed hostels.

## Gate Timings
Hostel entry gates close at 10:30 PM and reopen at 6:00 AM. Temporary changes for examinations, events, emergencies, or holidays require a dated Hostel Office notice.

## Late Entry
A resident expecting to arrive after 10:30 PM must request warden authorization before closing and provide the reason and expected arrival time. Security records authorized late entry. Repeated late entry receives human review rather than an automatic penalty.

## Emergency Access
Emergency entry is not refused solely because the gate is closed. Residents should contact hostel security or the duty warden. Immediate danger or medical emergencies must be escalated to campus security or local emergency services.

## Visitors
Visitors must register with security, show valid identification, remain in permitted areas, and leave by 8:00 PM. Overnight visitors require written Hostel Office authorization.

## Exceptions and Appeals
Students with recurring academic, employment, medical, accessibility, or transport needs may request a standing exception. A denied exception may be referred to the Hostel Administrator for human review.

## Privacy and Audit
Late-entry and visitor registers are restricted safety records. Authorizations and exceptions must be timestamped and retained under institutional record rules.
