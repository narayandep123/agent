## Scope
This policy governs issuance and replacement of student identity cards.

## Replacement
A lost or damaged ID card can be replaced after reporting the loss and paying the replacement fee. A bonafide or enrolment proof may be required for verification.

## Use
The ID card is required for library access, examinations, and claiming booked seats. It must not be shared or altered.
