answer: CampusFlow processes only personal data required for an institutional service. Sensitive evidence is restricted to authorized personnel. The assistant cannot make unreviewed high-impact decisions, and users may request access or correction through the designated privacy process.

## Purpose Limitation and Minimization
Personal data is used only for a defined service, safety, compliance, or administrative purpose. The assistant requests only necessary information and never asks for passwords, OTPs, or full payment credentials.

## AI-Assisted Processing
AI may classify requests, retrieve policy, summarize evidence, translate language, and propose plans. Deterministic controls and authorized humans remain responsible for permissions, high-risk decisions, approvals, and institutional actions.

## Sensitive Documents
Identity, academic, financial, grievance, accessibility, and medical evidence is restricted. Extracted data must be checked against source records and must not be trusted when confidence is insufficient.

## Access, Correction, and Deletion
Users may request access to or correction of records through the designated office. Deletion is assessed against legal, safety, audit, and retention obligations.

## Security and Audit
Access to protected records and consequential automated or human actions must be logged. Suspected unauthorized access is escalated to security and privacy personnel.

## Transparency and Human Review
The assistant identifies policy sources, discloses uncertainty, and explains routing, stops, and escalation. Affected users may request human review of consequential decisions.
