## Scope
This policy defines library operating hours and access rules relevant to seat bookings.

## Operating Hours
Libraries operate Monday to Saturday from early morning to late evening. They remain closed on Sundays and institutional holidays, when no seat bookings are accepted.

## Access
A valid student or staff ID is required to enter the library and to claim a booked seat within fifteen minutes of the slot start time.
