## Scope
This policy governs air conditioning, heating, and ventilation systems across classrooms, laboratories, and administrative offices.

## Reporting Faults
Report a broken or non-cooling air conditioner with the room number and floor. During peak summer months, non-functional cooling in occupied teaching rooms is prioritised.

## Service Standard
AC faults are logged as low-risk maintenance tickets and inspected within 48 hours. Complete cooling failure in an exam hall is escalated the same day.
