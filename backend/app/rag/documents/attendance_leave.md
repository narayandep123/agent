answer: Students should maintain at least 75% attendance in each registered course. Documented medical, official-duty, bereavement, or accessibility-related absences may be reviewed for relief. The assistant can route a request but cannot alter attendance or approve condonation.

## Attendance Requirement
Students should maintain at least 75% attendance in each registered course unless a published programme rule sets a higher threshold. Attendance is calculated from sessions conducted up to the applicable review date.

## Reporting an Absence
An absence should be reported through the designated academic channel as soon as practical. The request must identify the course, dates, reason, and supporting evidence where required.

## Supported Leave Categories
Medical treatment, bereavement, approved institutional duty, recognized representation, accessibility accommodation, and exceptional circumstances may be considered. Evidence does not automatically guarantee relief.

## Shortage Review
Students below the threshold may submit a shortage-review request before the published deadline. The department and authorized academic officer evaluate the evidence. Faculty and the assistant cannot silently modify recorded attendance.

## Record Correction
A suspected marking error must identify the course, date, and session. The instructor verifies the source record before correction, and the change remains auditable.

## Appeal
A student may appeal a shortage decision through the Academic Office within five working days of notification. An authorized human committee decides the appeal.
