## Scope
This policy covers hostel room allotment, stay rules, and reporting of in-room facility issues.

## Facility Issues
Maintenance problems inside hostel rooms, such as a broken fan or water cooler on a floor, are reported through the facilities maintenance process with the block and floor.

## Conduct
Residents must follow curfew and visitor rules. Violations are handled under the Institutional Conduct Policy.
