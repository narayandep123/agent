answer: Scholarships are offered on merit and need. You'll need to meet the minimum attendance and grade average, and need-based awards also ask for an income certificate. Applications usually need a bonafide certificate, your latest marksheet, and bank details.

## Scope
This policy defines eligibility and documentation for merit and need-based scholarships.

## Eligibility
Applicants must maintain the minimum required attendance and grade average. Need-based awards additionally require an income certificate.

## Supporting Documents
A scholarship application commonly requires a bonafide certificate confirming enrolment, the latest marksheet, and bank account details for disbursement.
