## Scope
This policy defines eligibility and documentation for merit and need-based scholarships.

## Eligibility
Applicants must maintain the minimum required attendance and grade average. Need-based awards additionally require an income certificate.

Applicants must be actively enrolled and must not be under academic or disciplinary suspension. Meeting the minimum criteria makes an application eligible for review but does not guarantee an award when available funds are limited.

## Supporting Documents
A scholarship application commonly requires a bonafide certificate confirming enrolment, the latest marksheet, and bank account details for disbursement.

Need-based applications also require a valid family-income certificate. The Scholarship Office may request identity or additional evidence when submitted records are incomplete or inconsistent.

## Application and Review
Applications must be submitted through the designated portal or office before the published scheme deadline. The Scholarship Committee verifies eligibility, ranks eligible applications under the scheme rules, and records its decision. Missing information is returned for correction rather than inferred by the assistant.

## Renewal and Disbursement
Renewal is assessed separately for each academic period and may require an updated marksheet, attendance record, and income certificate. Approved payments are made only to the student's verified bank account.

## Reconsideration
A student may request reconsideration within seven working days after receiving a decision by identifying the disputed criterion and attaching evidence. An authorized human reviewer decides reconsideration; the assistant cannot overturn or promise an award.

## Privacy
Income, bank, identity, and academic records are restricted to authorized reviewers and must not be exposed in public comments, chat summaries, or notifications.
