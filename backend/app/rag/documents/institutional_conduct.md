## Scope
This policy defines prohibited conduct and the integrity expectations that apply to every request made through campus services.

## Prohibited Conduct
Requests to forge, fake, or falsify documents, to bypass approvals, or to act without required permission are strictly prohibited and will be stopped.

## Enforcement
Any attempt to circumvent institutional controls is logged, denied, and may be referred to the disciplinary committee. No tool is executed for a conflicting request.
